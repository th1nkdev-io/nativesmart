# Vision

Nativesmart is Th1nkdev's mobile production infrastructure.

The project exists to accelerate high-quality mobile product delivery by combining shared design tokens, UI components, business components, starters, templates and generation tools for React Native and Flutter.

## Product focus

Nativesmart is optimized for products that need trust, clarity, resilience and repeated use:

- fintech
- mobile money
- marketplace
- SaaS
- delivery
- public services
- transactional apps
- offline-first apps

## Market lens

The design system must serve African and emerging markets with practical defaults:

- phone-first identity and onboarding
- mobile money payment flows
- intermittent connectivity
- low-end device tolerance
- localization-ready copy and formats
- accessibility and legibility
- strong transaction feedback

## Commercial direction

The internal foundation should later support premium starters, vertical templates, private licenses, enterprise support, integration services and customer-specific product accelerators.
